My idea for economy is that making actions as a [[Citizen|citizen]] or as a member of the [[Jury|jury]] (like welcoming new ones and explaining them the basics, accepting new [[Citizen|citizens]], voting, ...) gives you an amount of [[Graupel|money]], called [[Graupel]].

This money could be used to commission accepting artists and more via the [[Website|website]], giving them the money in exchange of services and / or could be used in the [[Snowfall City|map]] as a way of having additional content.

I also had the idea of giving an amount of [[Graupel|graupels]] to each [[Citizen|citizens]] every 1st of the month but I'm scared that people who doesn't really care about the nation ends up with a lot of money without having anything to do and ruins the economy.

There should be an easy way on the [[Website|dashboard]] to transfer money from one user to another so that other uses could be applied.

The idea of taxes were also talked about, but since this is a [[Is Demarchy the solution|demarchy]], the government itself does not profit of money and all money should be used by [[Citizen|citizens]] to get what they need and want, individually.
